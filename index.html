How to play

Each turn you get up to three rolls. Hold the dice you want to keep between rolls, then pick one box on the scorecard. Every box is used exactly once, and after 13 turns the highest total wins.

  • Upper section: add up the matching faces. Reach 63 for a +35 bonus.
  • Full House 25 · Small Straight (4 in a row) 30 · Large Straight 40 · Five of a Kind 50.
  • Each extra Five of a Kind is worth +100 if you already scored 50 there. It also acts as a joker: fill the matching upper box if it's open, otherwise any lower box at full value.
  • Keys: Space roll, 1–5 hold dice (own dice: 1–6 enter a face, Backspace remove one).
