How to play

Each turn you get up to three rolls. Tap dice to hold them between rolls, then pick one box on the scorecard to score. Every box is used exactly once; after 13 turns the highest total wins.

  • Upper section: sum of the matching faces. Reach 63 for a +35 bonus.
  • Full House 25 · Small Straight (4 in a row) 30 · Large Straight 40 · Five of a Kind 50.
  • Each extra Five of a Kind is worth +100 if you already scored 50 there, and acts as a joker: fill the matching upper box if it's open, otherwise any lower box at full value.
  • Keys: Space roll, 1–5 hold dice.
